# Research Plan

## Main Research Question

How do lifters currently combine workout history, sleep, nutrition, and recovery information when deciding how to train?

## User Interview Questions

1. How do you currently track workouts?
2. What information do you review before training?
3. How do you decide when to add weight?
4. What do you do after a poor workout?
5. Which recovery signals do you trust?
6. What is hardest to understand from your current tools?
7. What would make an automated recommendation feel trustworthy?

## Research Hypotheses

- Lifters have plenty of data but little explanation.
- Most progression decisions are based on memory or intuition.
- Users want recommendations, but they also want to see the reason.
- Exercise-specific insights are more useful than a generic readiness score.
- Trust depends on transparency and confidence labels.

## Research Output

Each interview should be summarized into:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| User type | Intermediate lifter |
| Current tools | Apple Health + Notes |
| Main problem | Cannot connect sleep to performance |
| Current workaround | Reviews recent journal pages |
| Desired outcome | Clear load recommendation |
| Trust requirement | Show the supporting sessions |

## Initial Product Implication

The product should not hide the analysis. Every recommendation should show:

- The signal used
- The comparison period
- The estimated effect
- The confidence level
- The limitation
