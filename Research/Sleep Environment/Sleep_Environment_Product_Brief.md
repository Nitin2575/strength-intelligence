# Sleep Environment Product Brief

## Product Concept

Add a sleep-and-environment layer to Strength Intelligence that connects nighttime conditions with next-day training performance.

## User Problem

A lifter may know that they slept poorly, but they may not know:

- What changed
- Whether the environment contributed
- Which workouts are most affected
- Whether they should adjust training
- What pattern is worth testing

## Proposed Experience

### Before Training

The product summarizes:

- Last night's sleep
- Personal recovery baseline
- Environmental differences
- Similar past sessions
- Suggested training adjustment

### After Training

The product records:

- Session completion
- Strength performance
- RPE
- Exercise-level outcomes

### Over Time

The product identifies patterns linking:

- Sleep environment
- Recovery
- Workout type
- Performance

## Example User Story

> As a lifter, I want to know whether my sleep environment is affecting my strongest training days so I can improve recovery and make better progression decisions.

## MVP Scope

The first version can use:

- Apple Health sleep data
- Workout journal data
- Manually entered room temperature
- Manually entered sleep quality
- Simple personal baselines

Future versions could support connected hardware data.

## Success Criteria

The feature should help the user answer:

1. Was last night different from normal?
2. Did similar nights affect previous workouts?
3. Which part of today's session may be affected?
4. What is the safest useful action?
5. How confident is the system?
